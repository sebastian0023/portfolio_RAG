# ADR-039: Chain of Responsibility for middleware

Status: Accepted (Phase 0)

## Context

Each validation/auth/rate stage needs clear ownership.

## Decision

Chain of Responsibility for middleware.

## Consequences

Explicit order and early termination; paid calls only after admission and authentication requirements pass.
