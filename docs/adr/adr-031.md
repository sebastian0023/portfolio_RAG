# ADR-031: Knowledge corpus is public-only

Status: Accepted (Phase 0)

## Context

Anything retrieved can be exposed to strangers.

## Decision

Knowledge corpus is public-only.

## Consequences

Never store phone numbers, addresses, private notes, credentials, or confidential employer/customer data; per-file public-safe review.
