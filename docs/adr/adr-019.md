# ADR-019: Blue/green chunks-<content-hash>; SSM active_index with short Lambda TTL; keep last 2–3

Status: Accepted (Phase 0)

## Context

Content updates and rollback must be cheap and reversible.

## Decision

Blue/green chunks-<content-hash>; SSM active_index with short Lambda TTL; keep last 2–3.

## Consequences

Promote only passing candidates; hash manifest includes config; prune only indexes that are neither active nor retained rollback targets.

[Source plan](https://app.notion.com/p/3efc9c3293d981c4ab1bcf5e2dad3d55)

## Amendment (Phase 5, ADR-053)

Index names are exactly `chunks-` plus 16 hex characters (S3 Vectors allows at most 63). Retention is the active index plus the two previous ones, and `prune` also spares anything active within the last 10 minutes or created within 24 hours.
